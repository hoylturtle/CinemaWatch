# Product Boundary v1.1

## Cinema Watch
Allowed: authorized cinema asset discovery, baseline, Missing/New/Weak/Moved/Unstable/Rogue and Device Hunt.
Not MVP: TMS/projector/HVAC control, HQ SaaS, invasive active network probing.

## Cinema Flow
Default: RF density, zone occupancy, crowd anomaly, aggregate heatmap, aggregate ticket/POS/gate joins.
Not default: persistent customer radio identity, cross-day recognition, individual journey reconstruction, RF-to-member/order identity linkage.

## Occupancy
RF device count is never treated as exact people count. Output should be an interval/confidence/anomaly risk based on per-auditorium calibration.
